The pattern

Progress had four different control styles stacked on one screen and orange doing three different jobs. This is the vocabulary the whole tab now uses.

Controls & colour

Three control shapes, each with one meaning. Nothing on this tab is orange unless pressing it changes what you are looking at.

Rules

  • The calendar was a wall of orange. Logged days are violet — they are a record of what happened, not something to press.
  • Chart lines are violet too. One orange thing per screen and it is the control you just touched.
  • Every metric switch is the same segmented control: Weight / Reps / Volume and Volume / Duration / Bodyweight are the same widget, not two.
  • Empty is a dashed box with one mono line — never a full-height card holding the words "No entries".
  • Delete never sits on a resting row. History is a list until you press Edit.

04 · History

Search is the kit's search field, sessions group by month, and each row is the same day row the plan uses: date chip, what it was, one mono line of what it cost. Tapping opens the session.

06 · Body — weight

Same tab, same controls. The chart line goes violet like every other chart, so the only orange thing on the screen is Log. BMI loses the seven-colour rainbow — one track, the healthy band marked once, your marker on it — and loses its Edit pill too: the height row already has a pencil, and two ways to do one thing is the thing the kit forbids. Entries get the History treatment: no × on a resting row.
